Energy and work

Remember the bank-account picture that clicked for you? Energy is the balance. Work is a transfer: when a force pushes something along, it moves energy into (or out of) that thing.

Key idea

Work done = force × distance moved in the direction of the force: W = F · d

So if I push a wall and it doesn’t move, I did no work?

Exactly. No distance, no transfer, so W = 0, even though your arms get tired. Your muscles use energy, but none of it goes into the wall. Now let’s see if it’s stuck. 👇
